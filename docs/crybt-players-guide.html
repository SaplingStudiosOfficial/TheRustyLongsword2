The Crybt

01Overview

The Crybt is a complete card game in its own right: a roguelike crawl scored like cribbage. You start with twenty health and keep crawling until you run out.

02Controls

03The Crawl

The pieces

ZoneWhat it holds
DeckA standard fifty-two card deck, shuffled at the start. When it empties, the discard pile is reshuffled back in.
HandFive cards drawn at the start of each encounter.
OfferingCards you sacrifice. This pile becomes your shopping money at the end of a crawl.
HeroOne card that fights alongside your hand and can be used in scoring.
MonsterThe card you are up against. Its attack power is its face value, with jacks, queens, and kings worth ten.
GraveyardMonsters you have beaten. You can pull a graveyard card out to replace your Hero, and each one adds to your end-of-crawl money.
DiscardEverything spent. Recycled into the deck when the deck runs dry.

One encounter, step by step

  1. Draw five. Your hand fills from the deck.
  2. Make an offering. Click one card from your hand to trash it into the Offering pile. You are now playing the encounter with four cards.
  3. Meet your Hero. A card is drawn face up. Choose Keep Hero, or Switch Hero to swap it for any card in your Graveyard, which discards the drawn Hero.
  4. Face the monster. A card flips up as the monster and its attack power is displayed.
  5. Build combinations. Click cards from your hand and your Hero to select them, then hit Confirm to score that exact group. Score as many separate groups as you can find.
  6. Resolve. Hit End Encounter. Damage is the monster's attack power minus your total score, never below zero.
Zero damage kills. If your score matches or beats the monster's attack power, you take nothing and the monster goes to your Graveyard, where it becomes a future Hero and end-of-crawl money. Falling short is not a loss, only a partial one: every point you scored is a point of damage you did not take.

04Scoring

Scoring is cribbage-flavoured with one strict rule attached: the exact cards you selected must themselves form the combination. The game does not hunt for hidden sub-combinations inside a sloppy selection. Selecting two, three, and four alongside a king is not a run, it is nothing.

An Ace is worth 1 point, never 11, anywhere in scoring. It counts as 1 toward a fifteen, and its rank of 1 puts it at the low end of a run, directly below a 2. There is no wraparound, so a King, Ace, 2 grouping does not form a run.

Combinations

CombinationRequirementPoints
FifteenThe whole selection totals exactly fifteen.2
PairTwo cards of the same rank.2
Three of a kindThree cards of the same rank.6
Four of a kindFour cards of the same rank.12
RunThree or more cards in consecutive rank.1 per card
Four-card flushExactly four cards, all one suit.4
Five-card flushExactly five cards, all one suit.5

Rules stack on the same selection. A five-card straight flush scores five for the run and five for the flush. Seven and eight together score two as a fifteen. A pair of sevens plus an ace totals fifteen for two, though it is not also a pair, because the pair rule needs every selected card to share a rank.

The same cards score only once. Each unique group of cards can be confirmed for points a single time per encounter. Re-selecting the identical set gets you nothing, so look for genuinely different groupings, not repeats.

05Crawls & the Boon Shop

Four encounters make a crawl. At the end of one, a final card is added to your Offering and the whole pile is cashed out:

  • Every combination of offering cards that totals fifteen pays two points, and here the game does search every subset for you.
  • Every pair among the offering cards pays two points.
  • Every card in your Graveyard pays one point.

Those points go straight into the Boon Shop.

Nothing carries over. Boons last exactly one crawl and then expire. Unspent points are wiped at the end of the shop. Spend everything, every time. Right now the Torch is the only purchase that changes play, so two points on a Torch is the whole optimal shop.

06Losing

Health only goes down. There is no healing in the current build, so a run is a question of how far you can stretch twenty points. At zero, the game ends and tells you which crawl you reached. That number is your score.

07Tips

Select precisely

Extra cards in a selection do not add points, they cancel the combination. Confirm small, exact groups rather than one big hopeful pile.

Feed the Graveyard

Every monster you beat is worth a point at the shop and becomes a Hero you can swap in later. Killing monsters compounds; surviving them does not.

Offer your fifteens

Offering points reward fifteens and pairs. When a hand card is useless for the encounter but pairs nicely with what you have already trashed, that is the one to sacrifice.
